About

I genuinely love organizing. I've always been drawn to creating order out of chaos.

After 30 years in corporate leadership, I know firsthand how much your environment affects your performance, your stress level, and your sense of control. A cluttered, chaotic home at the end of a demanding day doesn't just feel overwhelming. It costs you energy you don't have to spare.

That understanding drives everything I do. Not just the end result, but the entire process of clearing, simplifying, and creating order where there once was none. I know how much a disorganized space weighs on you, and how transformative it feels when that changes.

My approach is collaborative, thoughtful, and completely tailored to you. Together, we create systems that fit your routines and your life, so your space stays organized long after I leave.

At the end of the day, it's about more than just organizing. It's about creating a home that feels calm, functional, and truly yours.

If any of this resonates, I'd love to walk through your space together and see what's possible for you.

Maureen

How It Works

The Process

A simple, structured process from start to finish so you always know what to expect.

Consultation

We discuss your space, goals, and priorities. This complimentary 60-minute in-home visit sets the foundation for everything that follows.

Assessment

I evaluate your layout, storage, and pain points to develop a clear strategy tailored to your home.

Decluttering

Together we clear what's no longer serving you, thoughtfully, at your pace, with no judgment.

System Design

I create tailored organization solutions based on how you actually live, not an idealized version of it.

Implementation

We put everything in place. Every item finds a home, every system is set up and ready to use.

Follow-Up Support

Ongoing guidance to keep your space working long after our sessions end.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What happens during the free consultation?

We meet at your home for 60 minutes. I walk through your space, listen to what's working and what isn't, understand how you live, and outline a clear plan for next steps. There's no pressure and no commitment required.

Do I need to prepare anything before you arrive?

Not at all. Please don't clean up or organize before I come. I need to see your space exactly as it is. That's how I can give you the most honest and helpful assessment.

Will you make me get rid of things?

Never. Every decision is yours. My role is to help you see your belongings more clearly and create systems that work for your life, not to impose a minimalist ideal or make you feel judged.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the size of your space and the scope of the project. Most clients start to see meaningful transformation within the first few sessions. We'll discuss a realistic timeline during your consultation.

How does pricing work?

Sessions are billed at $100/hour with a 3-hour minimum. The initial consultation is completely complimentary. You'll always know exactly what to expect. No surprises.

What if I'm embarrassed about the state of my home?

Please don't be. I bring a calm, completely non-judgmental presence to every session. I've seen it all. I'm here to help you. You'd be surprised how quickly the process starts to feel manageable.

What areas of New York City do you serve?

I work throughout all five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island. I also travel to New Jersey and Connecticut for the right project, so if you're outside the city, don't hesitate to reach out. As a bonus, I'm happy to drop donations at a nearby charity or thrift store if it's walkable. One less errand for you.

Can you help with a parent's home or a major life transition?

Yes. This is one of the most requested and most needed services I offer. Whether a parent is moving into a facility, downsizing, or you're managing an estate, I bring calm, compassion, and a clear process to one of life's most difficult transitions.
